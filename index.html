Ethiopian Fellowship in CR

Version 7.0

Welcome to the central management hub for the Ethiopian Fellowship in the Czech Republic.

This application was designed to serve as a unified platform bridging our communities across Prague, Brno, and beyond. It empowers our coordinators to manage directories, track financial contributions, schedule programs, and foster better communication through automated systems.

Our Mission: To gather, grow, and support the Ethiopian Christian community across the Czech Republic through worship, prayer, and fellowship.
